FAQ

Frequently Asked

Where can I buy ASA NTI?

You can buy directly from our online store on this website, or find us at select cultural events and heritage markets in Accra and Kumasi.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — we ship worldwide. Diaspora shipping typically takes 7–14 business days. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout.

Can I stock ASA NTI in my shop?

Absolutely. We work with independent retailers, cultural spaces, and gift shops. Get in touch via the form above and select "Wholesale / Stockist".

Is the game suitable for children?

ASA NTI is designed for ages 12 and up. Younger players can participate with adult guidance — it's a wonderful way to introduce children to Ghanaian heritage.

How long does a game take?

A single round takes 15–30 minutes. A full game (first to 10 points, the usual target) typically runs 45–90 minutes depending on player count and experience.

Are expansions planned?

Yes — an expansion pack is in active development. Sign up for our newsletter or follow @asanticards to be first to know when it launches.
