How to Play

Gameplay at a Glance

Objective

Win the fifth and final trick of each hand to score points. The winner becomes dealer for the next hand. First to the target score — usually 10 points — wins.

Players & Cards

2–4 players. 39 cards are used per game from a 48-card deck — three standard suits plus a special fourth suit with just 3 cards.

Special Cards

The Golden Stool reigns supreme. The Okomfo, played on top of another card (not a Nana), forms a "Force King". The Queen Mother plays on any suit.

Turn Structure

Follow the lead suit if you can. The highest card of the led suit wins the trick. Play five tricks per hand — only the final trick scores points.
